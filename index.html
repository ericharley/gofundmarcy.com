Why Marcy?

Because friendship sometimes means intervening when a person refuses to spend a modest amount of money to dramatically improve an activity they do all the time.

The situation

Every year, Marcy selflessly goes places, takes photos, sends them to us, and gives the rest of us something to look at while we are sitting at home doing considerably less interesting things.

She visits faraway lands. She visits fascinating cities. She visits Minnesota.

And before every one of these journeys, she willingly enters the regular TSA security line like a person who has never heard of convenience.

The intervention

We have explained TSA PreCheck. We have described the shorter lines. We have mentioned the shoes. We have discussed the laptop situation.

None of this has overcome Marcy's deeply held belief that paying money for herself is an unnecessary extravagance.

So the community must act.
